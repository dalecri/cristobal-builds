FAQ

Questions, answered

The things people usually ask before starting.

How fast is "fast," really?

Most single-page sites are delivered in 3 to 5 days; tools and app conversions in 5 to 7. The timelines on each package are commitments, not estimates. If I can't hit one, I'll tell you before you pay anything.

What do you need from me to start?

Your text, logo, and any photos you want included. Don't have copy ready? I can help draft it. My communications background means words are part of the service.

Do I own the finished website?

Completely. You receive all the files and you own 100% of the code. No lock-in, no monthly fees to me, no holding your site hostage.

What does "AI-accelerated" mean for my project?

It means I design the experience and direct the build using modern AI tools. I personally test every detail before delivery, so you get custom work faster and more affordably with a human reviewing everything.

What if I need changes after delivery?

Each package includes revisions during the build. After delivery, small tweaks are always a quick message away. I'll quote them fairly.

Can you work with my existing branding?

Yes. Send me your logo, colors, and fonts and I'll match them. No branding yet? I can propose a simple, clean look as part of the build.

Let's build something for your business.

Tell me what you're dreaming of, even if it's just a rough idea. I'll reply honestly about whether it's a fit, what it would take, and what it would cost. No pressure, no sales pitch.
